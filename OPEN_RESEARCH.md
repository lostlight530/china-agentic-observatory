Status: durable open-research production guide
Scope: repository-level research positioning, longitudinal research-production method, scholarly-metadata boundaries, and semantic-drift governance

Repository positioning follows its declared purpose, implemented or studied research objects, and applicable public contracts. Existing canonical positioning remains unchanged.

Repository-owned workflows may implement research methods and produce bounded observations. Their substantive research role remains intact; the execution mechanism alone does not establish a research domain or scientific validity.

仓库现有定位保持不变；自有工作流的科研作用保留，执行机制本身不构成研究领域或科学有效性的证明

## Longitudinal research-production method

The existing Daily/Weekly/Monthly system remains the canonical cadence. This guide adds a common research-question layer without replacing that cadence.

For each substantive research unit, make recoverable where applicable:

1. research question;
2. falsifiable hypothesis or bounded judgment;
3. source identities and independence;
4. issuer / geographic / jurisdictional identity;
5. event/publication/effective/observation/transition dates;
6. policy, standards, project, or implementation lifecycle state;
7. raw observations;
8. counterexample or disconfirming evidence;
9. interpretation separated from official/project fact and external claim;
10. research increment and retest condition.

A Daily may validly conclude `NO MATERIAL CHANGE`. Weekly/Monthly work may preserve gaps, uncertainty, or no-transition findings. Cadence completion does not manufacture national deployment, adoption, interoperability, or maturity.

- issuer identity

- effective date
- implementation evidence
- maturity state
- source grade

Use `METHODOLOGY.md`, `DAILY_SOP.md`, `WEEKLY_SOP.md`, and `MONTHLY_SOP.md` as the detailed operational contracts

China-specific evidence must preserve issuer, jurisdiction, formal policy/standard lifecycle, project maturity, and implementation evidence. Official publication proves only what that publication establishes within its scope.

## Evidence and source discipline

```text
policy target != implemented capability
standards plan != published standard
published standard != implemented interoperability
approval display != exact approval time
case implementation != nationwide adoption
project existence != capability validation
observation date != transition date
same upstream source != independent corroboration
```

Repository DOI, archive records, prior reports, and self-citations are not C0–C6 external evidence.

## Scholarly metadata discipline

Before future metadata publication, preserve Canonical Type, One-line Positioning, Primary Domains, Non-goals, accurate structured subjects where supported, and 5–7 defining keywords. Avoid geography, astronomy, solar-observatory, remote-sensing, or generic national-index wording that distorts the repository's actual research purpose.

## Shadow classification

Candidate title + abstract/description may be checked against downstream topic/keyword inference.

```text
ALIGNED
PARTIALLY_ALIGNED
MISCLASSIFIED
CLASSIFIER_NOISE
```

Execution state is separately `RUN` or `NOT_RUN`. Repair owning metadata only for genuine upstream ambiguity; otherwise record classifier noise.

## Semantic drift audit

Periodically compare canonical positioning with `CITATION.cff`, CodeMeta, archive/DOI metadata, OpenAIRE, and OpenAlex.

- **CANONICAL_DRIFT**
- **TRANSPORT_DRIFT**
- **DERIVATION_DRIFT**
- **VERSION_SKEW**

`DERIVATION_DRIFT != REPOSITORY_DEFECT`.

Use `OPEN_RESEARCH.md` for research-method/positioning changes and `RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md` for bounded research units that need the common question/counterexample/increment structure. Existing Daily/Weekly/Monthly SOPs remain the cadence owner.
