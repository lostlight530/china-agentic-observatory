## What a lawful future promotion would require
- STANDARD-IDENTITY: exact issuer, object ID, revision, title, publication authority and link at a verifiable date
- MATURITY-TRANSITION: source-supported prior and new maturity values plus observed or published effective date, not inferred chronology
- TEXT-ACCESS: separately mark portal metadata, abstract, full normative text and inaccessible TIES material
- CLAUSE-CROSSWALK: only map clauses whose original full text was actually retrieved, with version and scope
- IMPLEMENTATION: identified implementation/system/version and evidence that the normative requirement is expressed in its contract
- CONFORMANCE: fixed verifier, version, test input, runtime receipt, limitations and an explicitly independent authority for the claim
- ABSENCE: a scoped negative search stays scoped, rather than becoming a statement of national absence
- HISTORICAL: later source status updates current interpretation but never rewrite earlier source-time observations
- All runtime/conformance and clause-level restricted-text tests remain NOT_EXECUTED / NOT_AVAILABLE in this reconciliation
