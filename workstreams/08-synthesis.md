# C8｜Cross-Workstream Synthesis and Judgment Revision / 跨任务综合与判断修正

C8 is the China Observatory's durable synthesis layer. It combines bounded findings from C1–C7 while preserving institutional level, lifecycle state, geography, date, source lineage, and uncertainty.

C8 does not create stronger evidence simply by combining multiple workstream summaries.

## Synthesis responsibilities

- normalize entity identity, Chinese/English terminology, formal status and relevant dates;
- separate national, ministry/regulator, provincial/local, industry/alliance, company/project, and research-institution evidence;
- deduplicate shared source lineages across C1–C7;
- distinguish policy/standard intent from implementation/deployment evidence;
- compare China-specific and global objects only at compatible layers;
- preserve counterevidence, conflicting official states, and unresolved transition dates;
- record what changed in a prior observatory judgment and which new evidence caused the revision.

## Evidence independence

```text
same official notice repeated by multiple outlets != multiple independent sources
company announcement + media quoting company != independent corroboration
policy + implementation claim != implementation evidence unless deployment is observed
multiple workstreams citing one source lineage != multi-source confirmation
```

Independent support should come from genuinely distinct competent sources, implementations, measured populations, standards records, research surfaces, or other independent observation channels.

## Lifecycle reconciliation

When current sources show a state but do not reveal an exact transition time, retain the distinction:

```text
observed current state
!= known exact transition timestamp
```

Do not backdate approval, publication, implementation, restructuring, or deprecation merely because a later page displays that state.

## Conflict handling

If evidence disagrees:

1. identify the exact proposition/object;
2. identify institutional level and authority of each source;
3. separate version/date differences from genuine contradiction;
4. preserve both sides where no stronger source resolves the conflict;
5. narrow the conclusion to the common supported core;
6. use `UNCERTAIN` where a stronger lifecycle or nationwide claim cannot be established.

## China–global synthesis

C8 may record convergence/divergence with global trajectories, but comparison must preserve C7's same-layer rule. A difference in policy structure is not automatically a technical-performance gap; a difference in product maturity is not automatically a governance gap.

## Durable synthesis object

A strong synthesis exposes:

- proposition and scope/jurisdiction;
- contributing workstreams;
- source/institutional lineage;
- formal lifecycle state and project maturity;
- event/publication/effective/observation/known-transition dates as available;
- support, counterevidence and unresolved state;
- observatory analysis versus external fact/claim;
- explicit non-claims;
- reason for judgment revision.

## Relationship to periodic outputs

Periodic reports may use C8, but cadence, completeness, closure and production rules live in separate SOPs. This file defines the stable synthesis responsibility only.

C8 must not rewrite earlier point-in-time observations because later evidence changes current interpretation.
