# C1｜History, National Strategy, and Policy / 历史、国家战略与政策

C1 owns the long-range institutional and policy frame for China's AI development: research-system history, national strategy, State Council and ministry policy, regulatory/public-service governance, filing/registration systems, and the transition from policy intent to observable implementation.

## Core questions

- What institution issued the policy, under what authority and at what administrative level?
- Is the object a strategy, plan, guidance, draft, consultation, regulation, implementation measure, filing rule, pilot, or enforcement action?
- Which date is relevant: announcement/publication, effective date, implementation milestone, or repository observation?
- Does the text establish a target, a requirement, a mechanism, or an observed outcome?
- Is a local/provincial/sector initiative being incorrectly generalized into a national state?
- How does the current policy fit earlier Chinese AI/industrial/informatization strategy rather than only global AI vocabulary?

## Preferred evidence

Prefer official State Council, ministry, regulator, legislative, standards-administration, provincial/local government, and other competent public-authority sources for policy/status claims. Use scholarly/institutional histories for longer-run interpretation and independent research for implementation/effect analysis.

An official policy source is primary for the policy's existence and wording; it is not automatically evidence that implementation outcomes have already been achieved.

## Policy lifecycle boundaries

```text
policy goal != implemented capability
published plan != effective rule
approval display != exact approval time unless dated
pilot != nationwide deployment
local implementation != national implementation
official statement != independent performance evidence
```

Preserve jurisdiction, issuing body, legal/administrative level, scope, target population, and effective conditions.

## Handoff to C8

C1 contributes the exact policy/institutional object, authority level, lifecycle state, dates, scope/jurisdiction, implementation evidence if any, unresolved gaps, and the narrow policy implication supported.

C1 does not infer technical capability, standard conformance, or nationwide maturity solely from policy ambition.
