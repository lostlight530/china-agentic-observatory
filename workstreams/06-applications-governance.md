# C6｜Applications, Security Governance, and Social Impact / 应用、安全治理与社会影响

C6 tracks AI/agent use in science, manufacturing, government, healthcare, finance, education, transport, consumer services and other sectors, together with security governance, labor, culture, public impact and rights/access questions.

## Core questions

- Is the observed item a pilot, procurement, internal deployment, public service, commercial product, sector platform, regulation-linked application, or independently measured outcome?
- What organization, location, user population and operational scope are actually evidenced?
- Does a claimed benefit or risk come from measured outcomes, a case study, stakeholder statement, survey, model estimate, or policy target?
- Which security/governance controls are documented, implemented, audited, or only recommended?
- Are effects local to one organization/sector or supported across a broader population?

## Preferred evidence

Prefer named implementing institutions, competent public authorities, original case/technical documentation, sector regulators, public procurement/deployment records, original surveys/datasets/research, incident/security disclosures, and independent impact studies.

A successful case study can establish that a named implementation exists; it does not establish a national or sector-wide rate.

## Application and impact boundaries

```text
pilot != scaled deployment
case study != national maturity
reported efficiency gain != independently measured productivity effect
security guidance != control effectiveness
policy-compliant process != technical safety proof
user adoption claim != population-wide adoption
local outcome != universal social effect
```

Preserve sector, geography, organization, population, time window, metric, comparator, and evidence method when discussing outcomes.

## Handoff to C8

C6 contributes the named application/governance object, implementation scale, relevant security/governance state, measured/reported outcome, population/scope, evidence basis, uncertainty, and limits on extrapolation.

C6 does not promote isolated projects, marketing cases, or policy demonstrations into nationwide capability or social-impact conclusions.
