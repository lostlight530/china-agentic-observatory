# C3｜Research, Models, Algorithms, and Evaluation / 科研、模型、算法与评测

C3 tracks China's public AI research and model-development surface: universities, laboratories, original papers, foundation models, multimodality, reasoning, scientific AI, robotics/embodied models, algorithmic methods, datasets/benchmarks, and public evaluation systems.

## Core questions

- What exact model/method/paper/version is being described and who produced it?
- Is a result from a paper, technical report, benchmark, public leaderboard, vendor evaluation, regulatory/evaluation platform, or deployed service?
- What data, harness, tool access, prompting/elicitation, configuration, comparator and metric define the result?
- Is the claimed capability a model property, a surrounding system property, or a workflow-specific result?
- Does a new result represent a new method/capability boundary or mainly scale, cost, latency, localization, or accessibility?

## Preferred evidence

Prefer original papers, university/lab releases, model/system cards, official project documentation, inspectable benchmark/evaluation methodology, public evaluation institutions, and independent research where available.

Company-reported performance is first-party evidence for the company's reported result; independent superiority or generalized reliability needs separate evidence.

## Research/evaluation boundaries

```text
paper published != result reproduced
benchmark score != universal capability
model release != production deployment
leaderboard rank != system-wide superiority
reasoning label != verified reasoning mechanism
model capability != agent-platform capability
```

Retain exact model/version/provider/deployment variant when material. Do not silently merge base, chat, reasoning, preview, API, app, quantized/distilled, domestic-hosted, or regional variants.

## Handoff to C8

C3 contributes the exact research/model object, claimed result, evaluation conditions, source provenance, independent support/counterevidence, release/maturity state, and explicit limits.

C3 does not infer policy maturity, industrial adoption, or national technical position from a single paper/model/leaderboard result.
