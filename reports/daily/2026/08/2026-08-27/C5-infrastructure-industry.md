# C5 — Infrastructure & Industry

Public National Data Administration cases show version/provenance as operational dataset infrastructure

One case describes dataset visibility, management, usability, traceability, version maintenance and on/off-shelf lifecycle operations

Another describes versioned corpus assets carrying source, version and responsible-person metadata

These cases strengthen the infrastructure judgment:

> versioning is not only documentation; it participates in dataset operations

**Boundary:** case implementations do not prove national interoperability
