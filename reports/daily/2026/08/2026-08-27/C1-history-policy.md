# C1 — History & Policy

The policy trajectory is becoming more lifecycle-oriented

The national high-quality-dataset implementation plan calls for management across collection, cleaning, processing, annotation, inspection, evaluation, iteration and audit

Today's new work-item evidence adds version management and provenance as a dedicated technical-file object

**Judgment:** policy emphasis is moving from dataset construction toward maintainable and reconstructable dataset state

**Boundary:** a work-list item is not a published technical file
