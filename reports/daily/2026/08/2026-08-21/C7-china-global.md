# C7｜China–Global Relationship

China’s trusted-data-space work provides a concrete policy-enforcement model around digital contracts, connectors, usage control and evidence return

Global data-space initiatives and policy-enforcement systems may expose analogous concepts, but comparison must remain layer-specific:

- contract semantics
- connector role
- usage-control policy
- behavior evidence
- interoperability

**Boundary:** similar architectural ideas do not establish protocol compatibility or mutual recognition
