# C6｜Applications, Security Governance, and Social Impact

The governance focus today moves from pre-circulation approval to post-access behavior

A mature data-use lifecycle must distinguish:

```text
permission to access
≠
permission to perform any operation
≠
proof that the allowed operation was actually followed
```

Digital contract, usage-control execution and behavior evidence create separate layers

**Durable candidate:** post-access governance is a first-class requirement for data used by automated AI systems
