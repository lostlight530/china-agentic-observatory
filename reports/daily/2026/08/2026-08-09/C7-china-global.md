# C7｜China–Global / 中国与全球

## New signal

ITU-T F.748.93 provides direct evidence that Chinese companies are participating in an international AI-agent interoperability work item while China’s own national and industry standards continue to evolve

## Refined comparison

```text
China domestic
GB/Z 185 → ASL → gateway / collaboration profiles

Global open ecosystem
MCP → A2A → discovery / identity / conformance work

International standards
ITU-T F.748.93 and related work items
```

These layers can interact. They should not be forced into a simple “China standard versus foreign standard” narrative
