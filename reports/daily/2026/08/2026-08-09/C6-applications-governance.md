# C6｜Applications & Governance / 应用与治理

## Finding

As interconnection moves from single-agent tools to multi-agent systems, security questions become authority questions

## Operational questions

```text
Who is the agent
Who authorized it
What intent was delegated
What scope was granted
How did authority propagate
Can it be revoked
Who can reconstruct the chain afterwards
```

## Judgment

ASL’s focus on trusted identity, connection, intent, and authorization is important because it treats multi-agent safety as a chain-of-trust problem rather than only a content-safety problem
