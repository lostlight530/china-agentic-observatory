# C4｜Agents & Open Source / 智能体与开源

## Finding

The standards stack is advancing faster than public evidence of unified implementation

## Analysis

Domestic open-source and commercial Agent platforms should now be examined against a clearer checklist:

- support for identity and discovery
- MCP/A2A or equivalent protocol boundaries
- gateway and policy layer
- intent preservation and delegation
- authorization and revocation
- audit and observability
- long-horizon recovery

A project should enter the durable map only when official repositories, releases, architecture documents, or papers show an architecture-level change
