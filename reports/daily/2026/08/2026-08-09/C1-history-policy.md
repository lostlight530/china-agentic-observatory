# C1｜History & Policy / 历史与政策

## Finding

The 2017 → 2023 → 2025 → 2026 policy arc remains stable, but the object of policy is becoming more operational

```text
national strategy
→ generative-AI governance
→ AI+ integration
→ agent policy
→ interconnection standards
→ industrial missions and deployment rules
```

## Judgment

The repository should now track not only whether a policy exists, but how it decomposes into standards, task lists, procurement, evaluation, and sector deployment

## Discipline

Policy intent is not evidence of implementation. Each downstream claim requires its own source and status
