# C1｜History, National Strategy, and Policy

W34 opens with an important policy-level refinement

The W33 lifecycle model left `validation → data feedback` open because the technical closure was not visible

National Data Administration materials show that this missing arrow is already an explicit policy and research object through:

- 模数共振机制与应用闭环研究
- 模型应用反馈数据回流机制
- 数据飞轮
- 高质量数据集应用验证

**Boundary:** policy recognition of the feedback problem is durable evidence of direction, not proof of technical implementation
