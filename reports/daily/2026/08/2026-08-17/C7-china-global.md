# C7｜China–Global Relationship

Today's China-specific signal is a policy / data-infrastructure framing, not a claim of national technical superiority

Useful global comparison should ask the same exact lifecycle questions:

- how application telemetry becomes feedback data
- how provenance is preserved
- how simulation and real-world data are distinguished
- how failure evidence affects retraining
- how privacy and safety constrain feedback reuse

The China observatory will not equate “data flywheel” language with an implemented loop, and will not compare policy documents directly against foreign product features without naming the layer
