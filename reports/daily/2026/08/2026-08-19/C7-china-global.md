# C7｜China–Global Relationship

Today's useful China–global comparison unit is **evidence portability**

China's current public policy language emphasizes standardized evaluation methods/tools and result mutual recognition for high-quality datasets

Global AI evaluation ecosystems more often expose benchmarks, model cards, attestations, audit artifacts, or vendor-specific evaluation records

These are not directly equivalent systems

The correct comparison is whether evaluation evidence preserves method, scope, version and applicability well enough to travel across organizations without losing meaning
