Portable quality evidence will eventually require machine-readable metadata rather than a screenshot or single score

Useful fields may need to preserve:

- dataset/version identity
- evaluation-method version
- tool version
- model/application context
- quality dimensions
- scope and limitations
- date / expiry / re-evaluation state

This is an engineering inference, not a published national schema
