# C6｜Applications, Security Governance, and Social Impact

Mutual recognition can reduce repeated evaluation cost, but it also increases blast radius if one evaluation is wrong or applied outside its scope

A reusable result therefore may need:

- scope
- validity period
- standard/tool version
- applicable model/application context
- revocation / re-evaluation conditions
- governance approval separate from quality approval

**Rule:** mutual recognition should not erase context or accountability
