## 0｜Executive Judgment / 核心判断

### 中文

截至 2026 年 8 月，中国人工智能已经从“模型与应用高速增长”进入“智能体技术底座、标准协议、安全治理和行业应用共同成形”的新阶段。2017 年国家战略、2023 年生成式人工智能治理、2025 年“人工智能+”行动与 2026 年智能体政策和互联标准，构成一条连续而清晰的制度路线。

今天建立独立中国观察站的核心原因，是中国路线已经不能被压缩成全球 Agent 生态中的一个区域案例。GB/Z 185.1—185.7 将总体架构、身份码、身份管理、描述、发现、交互和工具调用组合为国家级互联框架；TC260 又将部署安全延伸到评估、准备、部署、使用和停用全生命周期。中国正在同时建设技术、身份、互联、治理、场景和产业体系。

### English

By August 2026, China's AI system has moved beyond rapid model and application growth into a phase where agent infrastructure, standards, security governance, and sector deployment are forming together. The 2017 national strategy, 2023 generative-AI governance framework, 2025 “AI+” action, and 2026 agent policy and interconnection standards form a continuous institutional trajectory.

A separate China observatory is necessary because this system cannot be reduced to a regional case inside a global agent report. GB/Z 185.1–185.7 combines architecture, identity code, identity management, description, discovery, interaction, and tool invocation, while TC260 extends security guidance across the full deployment lifecycle.

## 2｜Historical Continuity / 历史连续性

China's AI trajectory can be understood as a progression from research and automation, through internet-scale industrial AI, to national strategy, generative-AI governance, and agentic standards:

```text
Automation and pattern recognition
→ Internet and platform AI
→ Deep learning industrialization
→ National AI strategy
→ Generative AI services and governance
→ AI+ integration
→ Agent identity, interconnection, deployment, and security
```

The stages overlap. Industrial automation, speech, vision, recommendation, robotics, and control remain active beneath the newer language-model and agent layers.

## 3｜Policy and Standards / 政策与标准

### 3.1 Agent development is now an explicit national policy object

The 8 May 2026 implementation opinion defines agents through perception, memory, decision, interaction, and execution, and organizes action around technical foundations, toolchains, standards, safety, applications, and innovation ecosystems.

### 3.2 Interconnection is structured as a seven-part national series

The GB/Z 185 series published on 22 May 2026 covers:

1. General architecture
2. Identity code
3. Identity management
4. Agent description
5. Agent discovery
6. Agent interaction
7. Agent tool invocation

The series indicates that identity and discovery are treated as foundational, not as optional implementation details.

### 3.3 Deployment security covers the full lifecycle

The TC260 guide published on 1 July 2026 addresses assessment, preparation, deployment, use, and retirement. This extends governance beyond content output toward high-permission operational systems.

### 3.4 Governance remains active rather than complete

The national-standard plan for an agent-model governance framework was assigned in May 2026 with a stated 12-month project cycle. It must remain labeled as a plan under development, not as a published standard.

## 4｜Research, Models, and Infrastructure / 科研、模型与基础设施

China's AI position is shaped by a combination of models, open-source engineering, domestic compute ecosystems, cloud infrastructure, industrial data, terminals, and application density. Model quality alone does not capture deployment capacity or supply-chain constraints.

The historical strength of engineering scale and application scenarios can accelerate feedback, but comparison still requires transparency about training, inference cost, hardware, data, evaluation, and operational boundaries.

## 5｜Agents, Open Source, and Industry / 智能体、开源与产业

The domestic agent landscape includes enterprise orchestration, research agents, coding agents, terminal agents, robotics, and open-source frameworks. Important projects should be tracked only when an official repository, release, architecture document, or paper shows a material change.

The central architectural question is whether Chinese platforms converge on a common control plane around contracts, policy, state, identity, tool access, and audit—or remain fragmented product stacks.

## 6｜Applications, Security, and Society / 应用、安全与社会

The 2026 implementation opinion names science, industry, consumption, public welfare, and social governance as application directions. At the same time, official explanations emphasize privacy leakage, unauthorized operations, and behavioral loss of control as risks associated with high autonomy and high permissions.

Therefore, large-scale application and full-lifecycle governance must be observed together rather than as separate narratives.

## 7｜China–Global Relationship / 国内外路线关系

Initial comparison:

- MCP primarily standardizes model/application access to tools, data, and context
- A2A primarily standardizes communication and task collaboration between independent agents
- China's GB/Z 185 series explicitly combines architecture, identity, discovery, interaction, and tool invocation across a national interconnection framework

These systems overlap but are not equivalent. Future reports must compare exact layers rather than treating all of them as one “agent protocol.”

China's policy framework also places industrial application, safety, standards, and ecosystem construction inside one coordinated program, while global development is more distributed across foundations, vendors, standards bodies, and open-source communities.

## 8｜Changed and Stable Judgments / 判断修正与稳定判断

### Initial judgment

China's 2026 agent trajectory should be analyzed as a coordinated policy–standards–industry–governance system, not merely as a collection of domestic models or open-source projects.

### Stable baseline

- Policy publication does not prove complete implementation
- A standards plan is not a current standard
- National standards do not automatically prove broad interoperability
- Company architecture does not represent the entire Chinese ecosystem
- China-global comparison must name the technical and governance layer
