## 0｜Executive Judgment / 核心判断

### 中文

截至观察截止时间，没有发现 8 月 9 日同日发布的 C0/C1 级新政策或国家标准足以改变前两日基线，但今天出现了一个更有长期价值的结构信号：中国智能体路线正在同时向国内标准纵深和国际标准参与两个方向展开

国内层面，GB/Z 185.1—185.7 已把总体架构、身份码、身份管理、描述、发现、交互和工具调用组成国家智能体互联骨架；中国信通院等发布的 ASL 又把可信身份、可信连接、可信意图和可信授权补到安全信任层；AIIA 7 月 30 日仍在推进多智能体协同、智能体网关技术要求和科学大模型基准测试方法的产业规范讨论

国际层面，ITU-T SG21 的 F.748.93《Framework and Requirements for AI Agent Interoperability》在 2026 年 7 月 17 日进入 **Consented** 状态，支持成员中包括蚂蚁集团、华为、阿里和中兴。这里必须校准状态：Consented 是 AAP 审批流程中的关键阶段，不应写成“国际标准已最终发布”

这组证据说明，今天最值得记录的不是一个新产品，而是一座正在形成的标准之桥：

```text
国家互联骨架
→ 可信身份与授权
→ 产业级多智能体协同与网关
→ 国际 Agent 互操作标准参与
→ 评测与部署方法
```

因此今天的核心判断是：**中国智能体基础设施正在从“国内标准成形”进一步走向“国内多层细化 + 国际标准参与并行”的阶段**

### English

No same-day C0/C1 policy or national-standard release found before the cut-off overturns the previous baseline. The stronger signal is structural: China’s agentic ecosystem is developing simultaneously inward through domestic standards and outward through participation in international interoperability work.

GB/Z 185.1–185.7 provides a national interconnection skeleton across architecture, identity, description, discovery, interaction, and tool invocation. CAICT’s ASL adds trusted identity, connection, intent, and authorization. AIIA continues work on multi-agent collaboration, agent-gateway technical requirements, and benchmark methods. At the international layer, ITU-T F.748.93 entered “Consented” status on 17 July 2026 with support from Ant Group, Huawei, Alibaba, and ZTE.

The status must remain precise: “Consented” is not the same as a finally published Recommendation. But it is meaningful evidence that Chinese institutions and companies are participating in the emerging international agent-interoperability layer.

## 3｜Policy and Standards / 政策与标准

### 3.1 National interconnection standards remain the domestic baseline

**FACT** — GB/Z 185.1—185.7 are current national standardization guiding technical documents covering general architecture, identity code, identity management, agent description, discovery, interaction, and tool invocation

**ANALYSIS** — The structure treats identity and discovery as infrastructure, not as application-specific add-ons

### 3.2 Trusted interconnection adds a security layer

**FACT** — CAICT and partners released ASL on 25 May 2026, describing trusted identity, trusted connection, trusted intent, and trusted authorization as core capabilities and positioning ASL as complementary to MCP and A2A

**ANALYSIS** — This is important because interconnection and trust are being developed as separate but composable problems

### 3.3 Industry specifications are moving closer to deployment

**FACT** — AIIA listed a third technical workshop on multi-agent collaboration and agent gateway requirements for 30 July 2026, alongside a second public workshop on scientific-model benchmark methods

**ANALYSIS** — The likely direction is a layered ecology in which national documents define core semantics while industry specifications refine gateways, collaboration, and evaluation. This remains an observation, not a completed architecture

### 3.4 International interoperability participation is now visible

**FACT** — ITU-T work item F.748.93, “Framework and Requirements for AI Agent Interoperability,” was marked Consented on 17 July 2026 under the AAP process. Supporting members listed by ITU include Ant Group, Huawei, Alibaba, and ZTE

**STATUS CALIBRATION** — Consented does not equal finally approved/published Recommendation. The repository therefore records this as an international-standardization milestone, not as a finished global standard

## 6｜Applications, Security, and Society / 应用、安全与社会

China’s agent policy continues to combine innovation and security rather than treating them as separate phases

As interconnection expands, the social and operational questions become sharper:

- who issues or recognizes agent identity
- how human authority is delegated
- how intent survives multi-agent handoffs
- how permissions are narrowed or revoked
- how incidents are audited across organizations

These questions are technical, governance, and institutional at the same time

## 7｜China–Global Relationship / 国内外路线关系

Today adds an important refinement

China–global comparison should not be written as:

```text
Chinese protocol vs foreign protocol
```

A better map is:

```text
Domestic national semantics
↕
Trusted interconnection and authorization
↕
Industry gateway and collaboration profiles
↕
Global open protocols such as MCP / A2A
↕
International standards work such as ITU-T F.748.93
```

Chinese companies can participate in international standardization while domestic national and industry standards continue to evolve. These paths are not mutually exclusive

## 8｜Changed and Stable Judgments / 判断修正与稳定判断

### New durable judgment / 新增长期判断

中国智能体标准化正在出现“国内多层细化 + 国际参与并行”的双向结构

### Strengthened judgment / 强化判断

标准状态校准必须成为中国仓的核心操作。`Consented`、`Under study`、技术规范研讨、国家标准现行，必须严格区分

### Stable judgments / 稳定判断

- 国家标准不自动证明现实互操作
- 产业规范研讨不等于正式发布
- 国际工作项进入 consent 阶段不等于最终 Recommendation
- 企业参与国际标准不代表完整国家路线
- 政策目标不等于已经实现的产业能力
