# C5｜Chips, Compute, Data, Terminals, and Industrial Infrastructure

The National Dataset Management Service Platform adds a concrete service layer above standards and dataset construction

Current official functions include dataset publishing, credential application, quality evaluation, search and demand publication, with connections to national data infrastructure and local systems

This creates an infrastructure path from dataset production toward discovery and circulation

**Boundary:** a national service platform in trial operation is not the same as a mature nationwide data market
