## 0｜Executive Judgment / 核心判断

W35 began by asking whether post-use evidence can change what happens next.

Seven real observation days now produce a much more explicit data-side lifecycle:

```text
24 Aug  post-use evidence becomes a lifecycle input
25 Aug  feedback return appears as an operating function
26 Aug  evaluation diagnosis → targeted dataset optimization
27 Aug  optimization receives dataset version / provenance memory
28 Aug  changed version → version-scoped quality evidence
29 Aug  official states rechecked; applicability / supersession remains open
30 Aug  scheduled midterm milestone arrives; completion and crosswalk evidence remain unproven
```

The official MIIT + National Data Administration notice for the 2026 `模数共振` action requires implementing regions and entities to submit stage-summary reports **by 2026-08-30**, after which the two departments will organize expert-supported midterm evaluation.

That date is a real program milestone. It must not be converted into a result that the source does not state.

```text
scheduled deadline reached
≠ all reports proven submitted
≠ midterm evaluation proven completed
≠ published evaluation results
≠ automated national feedback loop
```

Sunday rechecks also preserve the existing standards-state asymmetry:

```text
20256913-T-907 quality specification       → 正在审查
20262581-Z-907 evaluation-system document  → 正在起草
WG5 version / provenance document          → 2026 work-list item
```

No new formal version → quality-result applicability, re-evaluation, expiry or supersession crosswalk was found.

The Sunday conclusion is therefore not a new H12. It is a settlement:

> **China now has strong evidence for distinct feedback, evaluation, optimization, version/provenance and quality-governance objects; the national machine-readable linkage among them remains open.**
