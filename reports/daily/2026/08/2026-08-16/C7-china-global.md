# C7｜China–Global Relationship

W33 confirms that China–global comparison must remain layer-specific

China's current evidence is especially dense in national standards planning, data infrastructure, embodied-system architecture, training grounds, trustworthiness evaluation, and industrial validation

Global agent research this week is especially dense in discovery, identity, delegated authority, request provenance, and cross-domain trust

These are not mutually exclusive national “stacks” and should not be ranked as one protocol race

The useful comparison unit remains the exact layer: identity, discovery, communication, data, runtime, evaluation, physical validation, or governance
