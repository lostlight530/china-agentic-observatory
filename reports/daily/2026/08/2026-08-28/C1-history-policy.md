# C1 — History, National Strategy, Policy

## Finding

China's high-quality-dataset policy direction is moving from scale and construction toward lifecycle governance

W35 now has explicit signals for:

- feedback return
- evaluation-driven optimization
- version / provenance
- quality evaluation

The policy-level question is no longer whether data changes, but whether evidence attached to a dataset remains valid after that change

## Boundary

Policy and work-list alignment does not establish a mandatory national re-evaluation rule
