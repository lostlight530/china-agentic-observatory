# C7 — China–Global Relationship

## Comparative observation

The China line is developing around explicit dataset infrastructure objects:

```text
feedback
→ evaluation
→ targeted optimization
→ version / provenance
→ quality evaluation
```

Globally, adjacent work often appears through data lineage, model/data versioning, governance catalogs and MLOps controls rather than one unified national standards program

China's distinctive signal is the coexistence of national work items for quality evaluation, evaluation systems and version/provenance

## Boundary

Coexistence does not prove tighter implementation integration than global systems

The observatory should compare operational crosswalks and conformance evidence, not document counts alone
