## 2026-08-31 finding

The month-end infrastructure finding is the asymmetry among data lifecycle components:

```text
feedback / evaluation programs
version / provenance work
quality specification
quality-evaluation system
platform credentials / services
trusted-data-space controls
```

All exist as public objects, but they do not share one maturity clock or one published machine-readable crosswalk.

Infrastructure adjacency is therefore not interoperability evidence.
