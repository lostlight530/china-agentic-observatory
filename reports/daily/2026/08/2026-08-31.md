## 9｜Uncertainties and Watchlist / 不确定与观察点

W36 opens with five falsifiable hypotheses.

### H36-1 — Version-quality applicability becomes formal
A public standard / technical file / platform rule will define how exact dataset-version changes affect prior quality results.

**Falsifier:** mature national implementations consistently treat quality results as dataset-name-level properties with no version-specific applicability or exception logic.

### H36-2 — `模数共振` midterm outputs expose auditable correction lineage
Public midterm outputs will connect evaluation diagnosis to concrete data work, resulting dataset/model versions and follow-up evaluation.

**Falsifier:** midterm evidence remains only narrative ranking/reporting with no traceable lifecycle linkage.

### H36-3 — Standards-family maturity remains project-local
Related projects will continue to advance on different clocks rather than inheriting a family-level state.

**Falsifier:** an official package explicitly binds the family into one synchronized approval/publication state.

### H36-4 — Quality and usage-control evidence gain a formal crosswalk
Quality-result identifiers will become composable with rights, contract or usage-control objects without semantic collapse.

**Falsifier:** interoperable systems scale while keeping those objects permanently unrelated and unreferenced.

### H36-5 — Violation evidence produces explicit access review
Usage-control termination / violation evidence will trigger a documented credential, contract, access or mandatory-review state transition.

**Falsifier:** mature governance treats such evidence as archival only.
