# C6｜Applications, Security Governance & Society / 应用、安全治理与社会

## Result

The 2026 agent implementation opinion emphasizes high autonomy and high permission as sources of privacy leakage, unauthorized operations, and behavioral loss of control.

TC260’s 1 July deployment guide covers assessment, preparation, deployment, use, and retirement. A separate 29 July consultation on agent-interaction security and AI-browser security remains open until 12 August.

## Judgment

China’s agent governance is moving from generic content safety toward lifecycle, interaction, permission, and deployment controls.
