# C7｜China–Global Relationship / 中国与全球路线关系

## Result

China’s AI architecture now has two outward-facing layers in addition to domestic standards and industry work.

In July 2026, 29 countries signed the agreement establishing the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization, headquartered in Shanghai. The AI Cooperation Development Action Plan separately proposed eight actions spanning data, compute, open-source ecosystems, applications, talent, standards, security governance, and ethics.

## Analysis

China–global comparison should therefore include not only MCP/A2A-style technical interoperability, but also standards cooperation, capacity building, governance institutions, and public-infrastructure diplomacy.
