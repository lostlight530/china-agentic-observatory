# C2｜Standards, Protocols, Identity & Interconnection / 标准、协议、身份与互联

## Result

GB/Z 185.1–185.7 is confirmed as a current seven-part national agent-interconnection series published on 22 May 2026. It covers architecture, identity code, identity management, description, discovery, interaction, and tool invocation.

AIIA’s July agenda adds an industry layer around multi-agent collaboration and agent gateways. These workshops are specification-development activity, not equivalent to published national standards.

## Judgment

China’s interconnection stack is becoming layered:

```text
National architecture & identity
→ discovery & interaction
→ tool invocation
→ industry gateway & multi-agent specifications
```
