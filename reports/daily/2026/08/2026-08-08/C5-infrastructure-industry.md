# C5｜Infrastructure & Industry / 基础设施与产业

## Result

MIIT and six other departments launched the 2026 first batch of innovation-task challenges across six directions and 24 topics. AI-related directions include foundation technologies, application technologies, intelligent products and equipment, common support technologies, embodied intelligence, brain-inspired intelligence, and compute quality.

## Analysis

This is a move from general strategy toward mission-oriented engineering organization. It does not prove final industrial outcomes, but it shows where public industrial effort is being concentrated.
