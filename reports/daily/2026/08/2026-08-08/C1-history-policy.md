# C1｜History, Strategy & Policy / 历史、战略与政策

## Result

China’s current agent trajectory remains anchored in a continuous policy chain: 2017 national AI strategy → 2023 generative-AI governance → 2025 AI+ → 2026 agent application and innovation policy.

The 2026 implementation opinion defines agents through perception, memory, decision, interaction, and execution, and explicitly combines technical foundations, standards, safety, applications, and ecosystem construction.

## Judgment

Policy is increasingly moving from broad AI development toward operational agent systems, but policy objectives must not be confused with completed implementation.
