# C6｜Applications, Security Governance, and Social Impact

W35 distinguishes three post-use outcomes:

```text
FULFILLMENT
VIOLATION / TERMINATION
APPLICATION PERFORMANCE / FAILURE
```

Each may require different consequences: accept, restrict, review, recollect, relabel, re-evaluate, revoke access, or create new data work

**Durable boundary:** fulfillment proof or behavior logs do not establish semantic compliance or safe reuse by themselves
