# C3｜Research, Models, Algorithms, and Evaluation

W34 reinforces a model-development boundary

Application feedback can improve models only after provenance, quality and governance decisions determine what feedback is reusable

A model’s continued improvement therefore depends not only on data volume but on the lifecycle that classifies, evaluates, admits, constrains and later re-evaluates feedback

**Rule:** dynamic interaction data is not automatically safe or high-quality retraining data
