# C6｜Applications, Security Governance, and Social Impact

W34 demonstrates that post-access governance can be explicit

Digital contracts can carry usage expectations; usage-control mechanisms can validate and constrain operations; termination and fulfillment interfaces can create contract-linked evidence

But governance evidence must still be interpreted carefully

```text
policy execution
≠ legitimate purpose
≠ safe model behavior
≠ compliant derivative use
≠ complete lifecycle correction
```

**Open governance edge:** violation or termination evidence is not yet proven to propagate automatically into credential revocation, quality re-evaluation, data withdrawal or new training decisions
