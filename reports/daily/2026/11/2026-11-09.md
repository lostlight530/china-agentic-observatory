## 5｜Cross-System Analysis / 跨系统分析
AI-native lab 不只是将模型接入实验流程，而是将模型、实验过程与仪器能力共同设计，AutoDNA 是一个具有论文和代码数据入口的具体研究案例

但下列边界仍成立：

- instrument atomic service != portable Agent tool protocol
- multi-agent orchestration != portable delegated authority
- hardware step count != durable effect recovery
- exact DNA data recovery != general workflow rollback
- research result != independent replication
- issuer Demo != production deployment
- identified clinical mutation != approved diagnostic product
