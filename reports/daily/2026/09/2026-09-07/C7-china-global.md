# C7 — China and Global Route Relationship

Two clocks are visible today:

1. standards/security coordination: SG17 meeting and contribution process;
2. frontier capability: provider-declared research-agent acceleration.

China participates materially in the first through multiple Q16/17 contributions, while global frontier labs are moving on the second. Neither route supplies maturity to the other automatically.

Boundary: `international contribution != domestic implementation`; `frontier capability != governance maturity`.
