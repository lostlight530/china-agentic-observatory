# C3 — Research, Models and Evaluation

OpenAI published `Research acceleration: The view inside OpenAI` on 2026-09-06 and states it has reached its internally defined automated research-intern goal for well-defined research tasks under human direction.

The same source explicitly says humans still set research priorities, judge ideas/results and decide whether to scale, pause or deploy systems; it also warns that measurable coding/experiment indicators are difficult to map directly to research progress.

Classification: `EXTERNAL_CLAIM / PROVIDER-MEASURED CAPABILITY`.

Boundary: `provider research metric != independent scientific validation` and `research intern != autonomous research authority`.
