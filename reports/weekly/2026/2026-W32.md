## 3｜Most important weekly findings / 本周最重要发现

### A. China requires an independent observatory

Policy, national standards, industry alliances, infrastructure, open source, and sector deployment create a structure that cannot be reduced to a translated global stack

### B. The route is moving from scale toward structure

The important change is not simply larger models. Identity, interconnection, gateways, benchmarks, industrial tasks, lifecycle security, and cooperation mechanisms are becoming independent objects

### C. Standards are layered

GB/Z 185, ASL, AIIA technical work, and ITU participation sit at different governance and maturity levels. Treating them as one “Chinese agent protocol” would erase important distinctions

### D. International participation and domestic standards can coexist

F.748.93 shows that domestic standard-building does not imply isolation from international standardization

### E. Status calibration is a core China-specific research operation

Policy goal, current national standard, released protocol framework, workshop discussion, consented ITU item, and under-study item must remain distinct
