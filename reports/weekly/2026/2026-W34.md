## 0｜Current Weekly Judgment / 当前周级判断

W34 已经把 feedback lifecycle 从政策议程一路追到质量评测、结果互认、凭证、流通、数字合约、使用控制与履约证据

```text
APPLICATION / VALIDATION
→ FEEDBACK DATA
→ QUALITY GATE
→ PORTABLE RESULT / CREDENTIAL
→ CIRCULATION
→ DIGITAL CONTRACT
→ USAGE-CONTROL POLICY
→ EXECUTION
→ TERMINATION OR COMPLETION
→ FULFILLMENT EVIDENCE
→ COMPLIANCE / NEW FEEDBACK
```

当前最重要的未闭合边界是：**这些 post-use evidence 能否真正回流到质量评价、credential lifecycle、访问撤销和下一轮数据建设**

## 1｜W34 Working Hypotheses / 工作假设

- **H1 — Feedback-to-data becomes an explicit infrastructure problem**
- **H2 — Model-application feedback becomes a dataset quality gate**
- **H3 — Embodied feedback requires source-aware provenance**
- **H4 — Cross-system lifecycle mappings become necessary**
- **H5 — Application validation becomes backpressure on data construction**
- **H6 — Feedback governance becomes as important as feedback volume**
- **H7 — Quality evaluation becomes an explicit feedback gate**
- **H8 — Quality evidence becomes a portability object**
- **H9 — Quality evidence becomes a circulation input**
- **H10 — Digital contract and usage control become a post-circulation governance layer**
- **H11 — Fulfillment evidence becomes a post-circulation governance object**

### H11 falsifier

If large-scale trusted-data-space operation can remain auditable without contract-linked execution evidence, fulfillment proof, termination evidence, or an equivalent mechanism, H11 must weaken

## 4｜Durable Candidates / 耐久候选

```text
feedback data ≠ high-quality data
quality evaluation ≠ mutual recognition
identifier credential ≠ quality certificate
circulation approval ≠ unrestricted usage
digital contract ≠ policy execution
policy execution ≠ correct use
fulfillment proof ≠ semantic compliance
log hash ≠ complete audit context
termination signal ≠ downstream revocation propagation
trusted environment ≠ trusted outcome
```
