# China Agentic Observatory / 中国智能体前沿观察站

> **One repository, one day, one integrated research report**  
> **一仓一日一份完整研究报告**

A bilingual daily research observatory covering the history, policy, standards, science, open source, industry, infrastructure, and social reality of artificial intelligence in China through August 2026.

一个每日运行的中英双语中国人工智能研究观察站，覆盖从历史根基到 2026 年 8 月的政策、标准、科研、开源、产业、基础设施与社会现实。

## Mission / 使命

China's AI trajectory cannot be understood as a translated subset of the international ecosystem. It is shaped by national strategy, industrial capacity, application scale, standards, security governance, open-source communities, universities, laboratories, and domestic technology platforms.

中国人工智能路线不能被当作国际生态的中文翻译版。它由国家战略、完整产业体系、应用场景、标准协议、安全治理、开源社区、高校科研和技术平台共同塑造。

This repository searches and analyzes that system every day. It does not run or validate external projects.

## Scope / 范围

- 中国人工智能历史、基础研究与学科演进
- 国家政策、法律规则、备案制度和治理框架
- 国家标准、行业标准、团体标准和技术协议
- 大模型、生成式人工智能、智能体、具身智能和科学智能
- 芯片、算力、数据、云、终端、机器人与工业基础设施
- 华为、阿里、腾讯、字节、百度、讯飞、小米等产业路线
- Nexent、DeerFlow、Astron Agent、Qwen-Agent、UI-TARS、Trae Agent 等开源与工程生态
- 科研、工业、政务、医疗、金融、教育、交通、消费和社会治理场景
- 中国方案与全球模型、开源、MCP、A2A、标准和治理体系之间的关系

范围不限，但必须使用公开可核验来源并保持事实边界。

## Core Deliverables / 核心交付

Every day produces one integrated report:

```text
reports/daily/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md
```

Preserved research workstreams feed the report. Event ledgers, policy and standard cards, watchlists, history, and relationship maps are updated when required.

## Historical Baseline / 历史基线

China's modern AI trajectory moved from long-term automation, control, computing, pattern recognition, and machine intelligence research toward national strategy, large-scale industry, generative AI governance, the 2025 “AI+” action, and the 2026 agent policy and interconnection standards.

中国现代人工智能路线从自动化、控制、计算、模式识别和机器智能研究出发，逐步进入国家战略、产业规模化、生成式人工智能治理、2025 年“人工智能+”行动，以及 2026 年智能体政策与互联标准阶段。

See [`history/CHINA_AI_FROM_ZERO_TO_2026-08.md`](./history/CHINA_AI_FROM_ZERO_TO_2026-08.md).

## Why 7 August 2026 / 为什么是今天

On this date, the wider ten-repository system gains a separate China-facing observatory. The historical meaning of 7 August—correction after crisis and movement into strategic depth—is preserved as a methodological commitment, not as technical evidence.

今天，第十仓补齐中国外部世界模型。8 月 7 日所代表的纠错与战略纵深，被保留为研究方法承诺，而不是技术结论的替代品。

See [`ORIGIN.md`](./ORIGIN.md) and [`TEN_REPOSITORY_CONTEXT.md`](./TEN_REPOSITORY_CONTEXT.md).
