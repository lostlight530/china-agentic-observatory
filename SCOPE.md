# Scope / 研究范围

China Agentic Observatory is a bilingual, source-grounded observatory for China's artificial-intelligence system across history, policy, standards, research, models, open source, infrastructure, industry, applications, security/governance, and China–global interaction.

Its primary role is **public-source observation and lifecycle/status calibration**. It does not independently run every external model, project, platform, standard implementation, or industrial deployment it studies.

## Core research questions / 核心研究问题

1. **Formation** — how did a Chinese AI policy, research direction, standard, project, platform, infrastructure layer, or industry structure emerge?
2. **Authority** — which institution issued or owns the object, and at what national/ministry/regulator/local/industry/company/research level?
3. **Lifecycle** — is the object planned, drafted, consulted, reviewed, approved, published, effective, implemented, maintained, restructured, deprecated, or still unclear?
4. **Implementation** — what has actually been built, released, deployed, adopted, measured, or enforced rather than merely targeted?
5. **Scale** — is the evidence about one company/project/locality/sector or a broader national system?
6. **Comparison** — which China-specific mechanisms genuinely correspond to global models/standards/protocols, and which do not?
7. **Evidence** — what is official/project fact, attributed external claim, observatory analysis, or unresolved uncertainty?
8. **Revision** — what new evidence should change a previous judgment without rewriting the earlier observation?

## Included domains / 纳入领域

- history of China's computing, automation, AI research and institutional development;
- national strategy, ministry/regulator policy, law/rules, filing/registration and public governance;
- national, industry, local/group/association standards and technical documents;
- models, algorithms, multimodality, reasoning, scientific AI, robotics and embodied AI;
- agent frameworks/platforms, open source, memory/tools/workflows, MCP/A2A-related engineering;
- chips, compute, data, cloud, edge, terminals, robotics hardware and industrial infrastructure;
- manufacturing, science, government, healthcare, finance, education, transport and consumer applications;
- evaluation, safety/security governance, labor, culture, public impact and access;
- compatibility, divergence and interaction between China-specific and global AI/agentic systems.

## Independent observation principles / 独立观察原则

- do not use foreign terminology as the automatic ontology for Chinese policy/standard structures;
- do not treat policy targets as completed industrial/technical capability;
- do not use stars, funding, publicity, benchmark rank or isolated cases as substitutes for maturity evidence;
- do not equate one company's route with a national route;
- do not generalize a local/pilot/project outcome into national maturity without population-level evidence;
- do not treat an official source as authority for a proposition outside that source's institutional/technical scope;
- do not infer exact transition dates from a later page showing a current state.

## Not primary outputs / 非主要产物

The repository does not treat the following as sufficient research products by themselves:

- uncalibrated domestic/foreign news aggregation;
- policy slogan timelines without lifecycle/status analysis;
- product tutorials or procurement advice;
- code execution/benchmark reproduction presented as a substitute for public-source status research;
- investment advice or unsupported election/political forecasting;
- private/non-inspectable evidence;
- national-scale conclusions derived only from one vendor, locality, project, or pilot.

## Observation versus technical validation / 信息观察与技术验证边界

The observatory can verify that an official document, standard record, project release, repository, approval display, company statement, or research result publicly exists and can calibrate what that surface actually supports.

It does not automatically establish:

```text
policy target == implemented capability
standard published == adopted/conformant ecosystem
approval display == exact approval timestamp
project released == nationally mature deployment
company claim == independent performance proof
case implementation == nationwide interoperability
```

## Twin-observatory relationship

[`agentic-frontier-observatory`](https://github.com/lostlight530/agentic-frontier-observatory) provides a global comparison surface. Cross-observatory analysis should compare equivalent layers and preserve China-specific institutional/status vocabulary.

## Historical preservation

Later evidence can correct the current interpretation without changing what an earlier observation actually recorded. Point-in-time records retain their own source/date/status boundary.
