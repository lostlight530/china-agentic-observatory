## 1. 专业归属：这是 CS 的哪一类？ / Disciplinary Position

**中文** — 中国观察站的核心对象是**中国 AI / Agent 系统与技术制度的共同演进**：算法与研究、国产算力、数据和终端、工具／记忆架构、应用部署、治理机制及不同层级的标准。计算机科学关心这些系统的技术接口、运行能力和约束；制度研究则必须区分国务院／部委／监管机构、国标与指导文件、产业团体、企业、试点项目的合法权威边界。这个仓库不是全球观察站的翻译件，而是有中国本地对象本体和时间线的独立研究者。

**English** — China Observatory studies the **co-evolution of Chinese AI/agent systems and their technical institutions**: algorithms, domestic compute/data/terminals, agent engineering, applications, governance, and multilevel standards. Computer science analyzes interfaces, runtime behavior, and systems constraints; institutional and standards research distinguishes national bodies, ministries, projects, enterprises, pilots, and implementation authority. This is an independent China-specific research object, not a translation of the Global Observatory.

## 3. 人话解释 / In Plain Language

**中文** — 好比**专门研究中国 AI 技术与规则如何真正落地的调查台**：一个国家标准“正在起草”，并不意味着全国企业已经按它建设；一家企业发布了 Agent 产品，也不意味着形成了统一的产业标准。它把每一步的正式身份、发布日期、适用范围和实施证据弄清楚。

**English** — Think of a **research desk asking how AI technology and formal rules actually develop in China**. A national-standard draft is not nationwide deployment, and a company's agent product is not automatically an industry standard. The desk traces each object's issuer, lifecycle, jurisdiction, scope, and evidence of implementation.

## 4. 如何读懂而不夸大？ / Verification and Non-claim Boundaries

**中文** — 外部官方机构仅对其权限内的事实具有直接权威：官方存在性 ≠ 技术有效性；登记时间 ≠ 标准实施日；地方试点 ≠ 全国成熟度。国际推荐文件与中国国内登记的文字相似，也不能推导已经形成逐条对应关系。不得为了对齐英文 CS 概念丢失中国源文件本体。

**English** — First-party official sources are primary only for propositions within their authority: official existence is not engineering effectiveness; a registration date is not implementation; one pilot is not nationwide maturity. Similar vocabulary between an international recommendation and a domestic registry does not establish clause-level equivalence. China-specific document classes must not be collapsed into generic English CS labels.

## 5. 十仓中的独特贡献 / Place in the Ten-repository Research System

**中文** — 十仓的**中国本土事实与制度观测层**，与全球观察站是同级但研究对象不同的双站结构。它提供可核查的制度、技术与应用现实，让架构研究了解具体辖区和落地条件；它不代替五仓的实际代码验证。

**English** — It is the **China-specific observation and institutional calibration layer**. It operates alongside, not beneath, the Global Observatory, and supplies jurisdiction-sensitive technical context without becoming a test suite for the five core repositories.

## 6. 纪念与升华 / Commemoration and Wider Meaning
